## Representation and authority

Domain claims retain exact binding ID/revision/BindingDigest and binding-local
specification ID/EvidenceSpecificationDigest, PlanId/CampaignId, a closed logical
count unit, required AND members and explicitly preselected alternative groups.
Empty or duplicate claims, incompatible kind/count units and undeclared choices
reject. A release-record selection contains exactly one unit; files, transports
and attempts cannot define additional expected units. Independent review-principal
requirements are explicit, retained and bound to identity; later admission must
prove them. Construction does not grant trust. Members are bounded to 100000
per manifest, including alternative definitions; limit failures never truncate.

The separate canonical identity uses `eqm:v2:expected-selection-manifest` with
the approved zero delimiter and existing v2 NFC/JCS/set/numeric forms. Its root
contains source, kind, count_unit, required, alternatives and the review-principal
flag. All options remain represented even when unselected. No final obligation
digest is included, avoiding an S054 identity cycle. Frozen graph/contract/policy
roots, v1 identities, DTO/schema bytes, dependencies and Cargo.lock are unchanged.

Application preparation validates actual component digests and requirement/facet/
scope association against the same immutable graph and checked universe. Protected
expectations load a configured port once; candidate claims cannot replace its
response. A private application marker proves successful protected graph-provider
preparation; caller origin/mode tags cannot assert that marker. Development
supports explicit required AND claims; choosing an alternative requires the
protected graph and expected-selection operations. No reload, wall-clock capture,
execution or implicit selector-to-case inference occurs.

## Completeness and provenance

Engine binding retains the entire expected map in private evaluation context,
indexed by full pre-identity obligation coordinates. Omitted coordinate manifests
remain Unknown. Every binding/specification association composes with AND. Count
units and selected plan/campaign are homogeneous within a coordinate; distinct
logical IDs earn credit, never multiple source declarations of one unit. Exact
lineage and selected-member completeness guard prepared facet facts. Prior Unknown
coverage dominates Missing, and ordinary evaluation on an expectation-bound
universe cannot bypass the guard. Changed expectations invalidate carrier reuse.

## Controls and review

Domain controls cover all kind/count combinations, empty/duplicate claims,
release-reference cardinality, review independence and explicit alternatives.
Manifest controls execute independently specified literal bytes and independently
computed domain digest, permutations, every source axis, membership, max-u64
revision, unselected option definitions, choice and principal-independence changes.

Actual application preparation controls pair passing completeness with omitted
case/copied-report negatives; foreign plan/campaign/component/case/revision,
changed-manifest carrier, missing maps, protected provider load/equality, raw
protected-tag rejection, Unknown plus waiver, frozen alternative versus observed
choice, and real two-local-spec AND/ambiguous-unit cases. The real loaded policy
uses a synthetic count two: two distinct cases satisfy the guarded facet; the
same case under two declarations remains Missing. Graph/scope/profile/revision
substitution has a valid same-ingress positive control. Asserted facet facts
exercise local guard semantics, not live evidence or cryptographic acceptance.

Separate review found three material candidate gaps before acceptance: unverified
protected graph provenance, Unknown-to-waivable-Missing downgrade, and source
tuple/mixed-unit count inflation. They were repaired with paired controls. The
protected-only alternative interpretation was also clarified before repair.
Final separate allocated source re-review and all allocated gates passed.
