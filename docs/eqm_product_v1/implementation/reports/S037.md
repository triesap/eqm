## Scope

DEV-009 allocates a scenario with an exact identity and revision, participant
occurrences keyed by a unique occurrence ID with an explicit role and tagged
target/provider subject, and explicit directed dependencies between occurrence
IDs. Repeated target/provider references with different occurrence IDs remain
distinct; no set collapse.

## Representation

`crates/eqm_domain/src/scenario.rs` (new):

- `ScenarioSubject` is the tagged `Target(TargetId)` / `Provider(ProviderId)`
  subject.
- `ScenarioParticipant` carries `occurrence`, `role` and `subject`; every field
  is explicit and the participant orders by the complete member.
- `ScenarioDependency` is one directed `from`/`to` occurrence pair and rejects a
  self edge.
- `Scenario::new` requires at least one participant, rejects duplicate occurrence
  IDs, rejects a dependency naming an absent occurrence and rejects a duplicate
  dependency. Participants and dependencies are stored as canonical complete
  member sets. No target-ID set collapse occurs, so two occurrences of the same
  shipped target stay distinct.

`crates/eqm_domain/src/id.rs` adds `ScenarioId` and `OccurrenceId` through the
existing validated identifier family. The v2 `scenario` authored manifest
DTO/schema, discovery, conversion and complete graph integration are completed
by S037-R1 (RF48) below; exact multi-subject manifests remain the S038 concern.
