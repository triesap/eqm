Purpose and exact behavior changed:
RF68 reproduced that `WaiverPolicyV2Dto.maximum_days` used the generic positive
`u64` schema while its owning `eqm_domain::PositiveDays` is `NonZeroU32`. Literal
`4294967296` passed the graph schema but could not enter the owning
representation. The schema now enforces exactly `1..=4294967295`. The audit was
extended to every numeric member of the introduced graph/comparison and authored
v2 forms, and every faithfully schema-expressible owning bound was repaired.

`bounded_unsigned_schema` reuses the existing ADR-0013 canonical pair: the exact
integer form covers the safe interval and, only when the owning ceiling exceeds
`2^53-1`, the canonical decimal-string form covers the remainder. When both
bounds are safe only the integer form is admitted, because the real `WireUint`
rejects a small-value string. This preserves the number/decimal-string
transition, signed extension integers and the strict raw-token layer; no
permissive fallback or domain parser loosening was introduced.
