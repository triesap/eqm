Purpose and exact behavior changed:
The v2 semantic-graph and comparison-context schemas are no longer placeholder
`{"type":"array"}`/arbitrary-string bags. They are generated from typed
`schemars` carriers that mirror the manifest projections exactly: every graph
collection has its real member shape and nested union constraints, SHA
references use exact `sha256:<64 lowercase hex>` spelling, v2 integers use the
approved wire form, optional members are absent-only (never `null`), canonical
sets are unique arrays, and runner/scope/applicability/selector/extension unions
are closed tagged forms. The comparison root carries the exact four-member
semantic root plus only the schema marker; its profile selection uses exact
definition/revision and known/unknown member shapes. The two families are now
registered in `V2_PROTOCOL_SCHEMAS`. All v1 schema bytes are unchanged.

Spec/API/schema/DB/authority impact:
Additive public schema carriers in `eqm_protocol` and two regenerated v2
protocol schemas. No v1 schema/bytes/digest changed, no new production
dependency (only a test-only `jsonschema` dev-dependency already resolved in the
lock), and no domain/engine/manifest back-edge. The typed carriers add no trust
or admission behavior; raw duplicate/token and cross-field checks remain reader
obligations.
