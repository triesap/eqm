## Behavior and input-domain relationships

The projection covers the declared input domains of an `EvidenceSpecification`:
`id` (a local `EvidenceSpecId` unique within one binding); `kind` (closed
`EvidenceKind`, which determines whether a runner and a positive `minimum_count`
are required or forbidden); `requirements` (nonempty fully qualified
`FullRequirementId` values of the form `<unit>#<local>`) and `facets` (nonempty
closed `Facet` vocabulary); the kind-compatible `selector`; the normalized
positive `minimum_count`; the optional bounded `freshness` age ceiling; and
semantic `extensions`. These relationships are documented on the module.

Representation follows the S017 rules: revisions/counts/freshness use the v2
integer wire forms; requirements, facets and any applicability set are ordered by
complete canonical member bytes and exact duplicates are rejected; ordered
extension arrays keep their order; only `dev.equivalencematrix.display` is
excluded; no description/comment or parse-provenance field is invented. The v1
projection, bytes and hashes are unchanged, and no `eqm_manifest` ->
protocol/transport dependency was added.
